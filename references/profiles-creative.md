# Profile: creative (default)

Mode flag: `--mode creative` (CLI/engine default).

## Purpose

Frame Sigil-Forge as a **creative / focus tool**: clarify, compress, and
externalize a statement of intent as a durable multi-encoded symbol. Suitable
for journaling, habit cues, design, and offline symbolic craft.

## Engine behavior

**Identical** to practice mode. Mode changes packet `mode`, `framing_notes`, and
agent tone only — not geometry, digest, or stego.

## Agent tone

- Emphasize craft, design, compression, and verifiable artifacts.  
- Avoid “charge,” “cast,” “results magic,” or ritual-obligation language.  
- Describe channels as encoding layers, not metaphysical forces.  
- Celebrate offline completeness: procedural SVG + packet without image APIs.

## Packet framing (engine default text)

> Creative / focus tool: externalize and compress intent as a durable symbol.
> Methods are historical craft, symbolic encoding, and data embedding—not claims
> of supernatural efficacy.

## Do not

- Claim the sigil will cause outcomes or “manifest” events.  
- Soften harmful intents into creative metaphor — refuse instead.  
- Invent geometry; always use construct scripts.  
- Present AI polish as the master carrier if it breaks verify.

## Example deliverable summary

> Forged in **creative** mode. Master glyph at `…/glyph.svg`. Digest
> `…`. Channels: N applied, M skipped (see packet). Verify with
> `python3 scripts/sigil_forge.py verify …`. Methods are symbolic craft and
> multi-channel encoding only.
