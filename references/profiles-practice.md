# Profile: practice (opt-in)

Mode flag: `--mode practice`.

## Purpose

Same multi-channel construction as creative mode, with **practitioner-oriented
language** for operators who use sigils in personal symbolic practice. Optional
short personal-use notes (gaze, place, discard) are **operator-owned** suggestions
only — never efficacy guarantees.

## Engine behavior

**Identical** to creative mode. Only framing and agent prose differ.

## Agent tone

- Respect craft history (Spare, kamea) as **historical methods**, not proof.  
- Optional use notes may mention: quiet gaze, place the glyph where you will see
  it, discard or archive when the work is complete — as personal preferences.  
- Still **no** claims that the sigil causes external events, binds others, or
  replaces medical/legal help.  
- Prefer “you may choose to…” over imperatives that imply magical necessity.

## Packet framing (engine default text)

> Practice profile: same construction as creative mode. Optional personal notes
> (gaze, place, discard) are operator-owned. No efficacy claims; methods are craft
> history and multi-channel encoding only.

## Optional personal-use note template (agent-only)

When the user opts into practice notes, keep them short and non-prescriptive:

1. Keep the master SVG/PNG and packet for verify.  
2. If you use a polished image, treat it as presentation; master remains carrier.  
3. Place, view, or retire the symbol on your own schedule.  
4. Do not use practice framing to encode harm or control of others.

## Do not

- Add liturgy, banishing systems, or results tracking as v1 product.  
- Collapse practice mode into Enochian authority seals (`distinction-enochian.md`).  
- Promise outcomes (“this will make X happen”).  
- Store secret practice logs in `references/` or public stego by default.
